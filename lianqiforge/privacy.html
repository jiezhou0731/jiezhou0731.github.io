Pixel Forge: Idle Immortal Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 (updated for optional rewarded videos)

Pixel Forge: Idle Immortal ("the game") is made by Jie Zhou. In short: we collect nothing ourselves. The only third-party service is Google AdMob, which shows optional rewarded videos. We do not track you.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it sends us no name, email, location, contacts, usage data or crash reports. It never asks for permission to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or to Google.

Game progress

Your progress (realm, gear, spirit stones, jade, ore, pets, techniques, purchases and settings) is saved only on your device. Nobody else can see it, including us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob: you choose to watch one in exchange for a small in-game bonus. There are no banners and no interstitial ads. To serve and measure these videos, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information (such as model, OS version and IP address), ad interaction data and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns every video into an instant reward, so no ads are requested at all.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
