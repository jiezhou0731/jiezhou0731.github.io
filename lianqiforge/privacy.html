LianQi: Pixel Forge Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

LianQi: Pixel Forge ("the game") is made by Jie Zhou. In short: the game collects no data at all. It has no ads, no analytics and no third-party services.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not collect or send your name, email, location, contacts, device or advertising identifiers, usage data or crash reports. It never asks for permission to track you.

Game progress

Your progress (realm, gear, spirit stones, jade, ore, pets, techniques, purchases and settings) is saved only on your device. Nobody else can see it, including us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
