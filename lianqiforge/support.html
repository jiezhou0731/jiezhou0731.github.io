How do I play?

Tap Forge to put spirit ore into the cauldron. About one strike in four forges a piece of gear: compare it with what you wear, then equip it or salvage it for spirit stones. Your cultivator fights monsters on their own in the battle strip at the top, and kills and meditation fill your XP bar. Spirit ore regrows over time.

How do I reach the next realm?

The layers inside a realm come on their own as your XP fills. At the ninth layer a Tribulation button appears: win the fight against your Inner Demon to break through to the next realm. If a boss is too strong, forge better gear, raise your pets and techniques, then tap Fight Boss to try again.

What happens while I'm away?

Spirit stones keep coming in while you are away, plus up to 12 hours of XP and ore, and a running cauldron upgrade finishes on its own.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game. Purchases that were interrupted are delivered automatically the next time the game starts.

I reinstalled or got a new device

Open the Shop tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore Purchases. This restores the Starter Pack's Qilin pet. Jade, the Monthly Card and other progress are consumable and saved only on the device they were bought or earned on, so they cannot be restored.

Language

The game starts in English. Tap the 中 button at the top right of the screen to switch to Simplified Chinese (and EN to switch back).
