How do I play?

Drag from one of your cells to another cell to send your virions. Capture neutral and enemy cells, upgrade or convert your cells, and wipe out the enemy strain. Each chapter is five stages in a row; capturing cells earns gene XP, and every level-up lets you pick one of three mutations that last for the whole chapter.

Cell types

Replicators produce virions, Spike Towers shoot passing enemies, and Enzyme Forges boost your whole army's attack and defence.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game. Purchases that were interrupted are delivered automatically the next time the game starts.

I reinstalled or got a new device

Open the 商店 (Shop) tab, scroll to the bottom and tap 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases). This restores the Starter Pack unlock and the Growth Fund. Gems, coins, the Monthly Card, the Season Pass and other progress are saved only on the device they were bought or earned on.
