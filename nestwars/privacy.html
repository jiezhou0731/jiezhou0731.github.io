Nest Wars Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Nest Wars ("the game") is made by Jie Zhou. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

Game progress

Your progress (coins, gems, gear, talents, purchases, cleared chapters and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
