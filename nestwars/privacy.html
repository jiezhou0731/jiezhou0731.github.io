Nest Wars Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Nest Wars ("the game") is made by Jie Zhou. The short version: we do not collect or store any personal data ourselves. Optional rewarded video ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts or usage data, and we run no servers that receive data from the game.

Game progress

Your progress (coins, gems, gear, talents, purchases, cleared chapters and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and tracking

The game may offer optional rewarded video ads (for example to double a reward, revive or get a bonus). Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect information such as your IP address, device type and model, operating system, an advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) and ad interaction data, in order to deliver, measure and prevent fraud in ads. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, so AdMob receives no advertising identifier unless you have granted tracking. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying the No-Ads item in the shop removes the need to watch ads: the rewards are granted straight away.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
