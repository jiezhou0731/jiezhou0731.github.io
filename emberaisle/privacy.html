Privacy Policy暖光连锁超市 · Ember Aisle: Grocery Chain

Effective November 17, 2026

暖光连锁超市 (Ember Aisle: Grocery Chain, "the game") is a single-player store-management game made by Jie Zhou. The game itself does not collect personal data. It offers optional rewarded videos served by Google AdMob, and a one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase handled by Apple.

What the game collects

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. Your stores, staff, manager career, settings and whether you own Remove Ads are saved only on your device, and are removed when you delete the game. The developer does not receive any of this data.

Rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

Some buttons offer a bonus (for example the day's profit again, a free shelf refill or a head office grant) if you choose to watch a short video. Videos play only when you tap such a button; there are no banners and no interstitial ads. The videos are provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver and measure them, the SDK may collect device information (such as device model, operating system version, language and an approximate location derived from the IP address), usage data (such as ad views and taps) and diagnostic data (such as crash and performance logs). The game does not ask for permission to track you and cannot access the advertising identifier (IDFA), so it is not used to track you across apps. Google's use of this data is described in its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The game is free. A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase (non-consumable) makes every video button give its reward at once without a video. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; the game never sees your payment details. "Restore Purchases" in the game asks the App Store to bring the purchase back on a new device.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
