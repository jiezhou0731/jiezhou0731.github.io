暖光连锁超市 (Ember Aisle: Grocery Chain, "the game") is a single-player store-management game made by Jie Zhou. It does not collect any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no network features. Your stores, staff, manager career and settings are saved only on your device, and are removed when you delete the game.

Purchases

The game is free and has no in-app purchases.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.
