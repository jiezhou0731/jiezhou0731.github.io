How do I play?

Tap 炼器 (Forge) to put one spirit ore into the cauldron. About one strike in four forges a piece of gear: compare it with what you wear, then equip it or break it down into spirit stones. Your cultivator fights monsters on their own in the battle strip at the top, and kills and meditation fill your cultivation bar. Spirit ore regrows over time.

How do I reach the next realm?

The layers inside a realm come on their own as your cultivation fills. At the ninth layer, a 渡劫 (Tribulation) button appears: win the fight against your heart demon to break through to the next realm. If a stage boss is too strong, forge better gear, level your pets and techniques, then tap the challenge button to try again.

What happens while I'm away?

You earn up to 12 hours of offline rewards (spirit stones, cultivation and ore), and a running cauldron upgrade finishes on its own.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game. Purchases that were interrupted are delivered automatically the next time the game starts.

I reinstalled or got a new device

Open the 商店 (Shop) tab, scroll to the bottom and tap 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases). This restores the First Purchase Pack's Qilin pet and the No-Ads pass. Jade, the Month Card and other progress are saved only on the device they were bought or earned on.
