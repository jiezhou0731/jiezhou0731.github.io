TimedshotPrivacy · effective 23 November 2026

Short version: Timedshot, by Jie Zhou, collects nothing about you.

On your phone

Two things are saved, on the device only: your highest number of hits in one run, and whether sound is muted. Deleting the app deletes both. The app contains no advertising, no tracking or analytics code, no sign-up and nothing to buy inside it.

Game Center

When you are signed in to Game Center, each run's hit count is submitted to the "Most Hits" leaderboard. Apple runs that service under your Game Center settings and Apple's privacy policy. The developer sees only the public leaderboard, the same as any player.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.
