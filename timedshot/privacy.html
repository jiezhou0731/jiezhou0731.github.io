Privacy PolicyTimedshot

Effective November 23, 2026

Timedshot ("the game") is a single-player arcade game made by Jie Zhou. It does not collect any personal data.

What the game stores

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. Your best score and the sound setting are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the game. The developer receives none of this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the game sends your best score to its Game Center leaderboard so it can be ranked against other players. This is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings and Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy); the developer only sees the public leaderboard.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
