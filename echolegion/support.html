Echo Legion Support

Need help? Email jiezhou0731@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I play?

Tap Fight on the home page to start a chapter. Your hero moves and shoots on its own; you manage the cards. Tap a card in the bag to equip, use or sell it. A chapter has ten stages: survive each one, beat the elites on stages 3, 6 and 9 and the boss on stage 10.

What are echoes?

Every stage you clear is recorded. In the following stages, that recording comes back as an echo: a copy of your hero that walks the same path with the cards you held, fighting beside you. By the boss there are ten of you.

I bought something but did not receive it

Purchases are delivered as soon as the App Store confirms them. If the game was closed during a purchase, the item is delivered the next time you open the game. The First Purchase Pack and the Barrier Pack can be restored on a new device with Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Shop. If something is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
