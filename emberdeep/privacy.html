Emberdeep Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Emberdeep ("the game") is made by Jie Zhou. In short: the game itself collects no personal data and has no accounts or analytics. Its only third-party service is Google AdMob, which supplies the optional rewarded videos described below. Nothing is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information we collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts, and we run no analytics of our own. The game never asks for permission to track you. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob collects to serve the optional videos (see Advertising).

Game progress

Your progress (deepest floor, embers, Anvil upgrades, Codex and settings) is saved only on your device. Nobody else can see it, including us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). A video only plays when you tap a button that names its reward, such as an extra rune reroll, a second rise after falling, doubled embers or an Ember Vigil in the Shop. There are no banners and no pop-up ads. The one-time Remove Ads purchase gives every reward without the video.

To load and show these videos, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information (such as device model, operating system, IP address and, if allowed, an identifier for advertisers or vendor), advertising and usage data (such as which ads were shown and interacted with) and diagnostic data (such as crash and performance information). This data is used by Google to deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. The game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, so the advertising identifier is not available and the data is not used to track you. Google's handling of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
