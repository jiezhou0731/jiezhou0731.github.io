Emberdeep Support

Need help? Email jiezhou0731@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I play?

Tap Descend on the Camp tab. Your hero fights on their own; each floor has three waves of monsters, and every fifth floor ends with a boss. After each floor, pick one of three runes. Runes raise your stats or cast spells on their own, and picking a rune again levels it up, to level 5 at most.

What happens when my hero falls?

The run ends. Once per run you can rise again with half your health. Whatever embers you earned are banked when the run ends, and you can spend them at the Anvil on permanent upgrades that make every later run stronger.

Can I start deeper?

Yes. Every five floors you reach unlocks a deeper starting point on the Camp tab. Starting deeper also gives you a few rune picks right away.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game. Interrupted purchases are delivered automatically the next time the game starts.

I reinstalled or got a new device

Embers and progress are saved only on the device where you earned them, so they do not move to a new device or come back after deleting the game.

Refunds

Apple handles payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Language

The game starts in English. Switch to Simplified Chinese (or back) in Settings, the gear icon on the Camp tab.

Privacy

The game has no ads and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
