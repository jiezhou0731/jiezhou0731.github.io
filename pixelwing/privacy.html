Pixel Wing Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Pixel Wing ("the game") is made by Jie Zhou. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

Game progress

Your progress (gold, gems, planes, upgrades, cleared chapters and sound settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
