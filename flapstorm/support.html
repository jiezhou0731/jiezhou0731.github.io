Flap Storm Support

Need help? Email jiezhou0731@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I play?

Tap to flap and stay in the air. Your agent fires automatically at monsters. Collect gems to level up and pick one of three skills each time. Survive the stage and defeat the chapter boss to unlock the next chapter.

What does energy do?

Each run costs 5 energy. Energy refills by one point every 12 minutes, and you can top it up with gems a couple of times a day.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game. Purchases that were interrupted are delivered automatically the next time the game starts.

I reinstalled or got a new device

Open the 商店 (Shop) tab and tap 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases). This restores the Growth Fund. Gems, gold, the Starter Pack, the Monthly Card and other progress are saved only on the device they were bought or earned on.
