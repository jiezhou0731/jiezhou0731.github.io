How do I play?

Drag a model's portrait from the bottom bar onto a free spot in the tower. Each room earns coins (金币) or fame (人气) on its own. Recruit new models on the 招募 (Recruit) tab, level them up from their profile, and build new floors when you have enough coins and fame.

What are the things I can tap in the tower?

Fan cheers, live-stream gifts, photo-shoot rings, runway beats, paparazzi pop-ups, falling stars and the sponsor blimp all give bonus rewards. Tapping them back to back builds a combo that makes every tap worth more. Tap any number or icon in the game to see what it does.

How do chapters work?

Each chapter is a new city. Reach the chapter's coins-per-second goal to clear it, collect the medal and star gems, then move on to the next city or keep playing for bonus stars.

I bought something but did not receive it

Purchases are delivered as soon as the App Store confirms them. If the game was closed during a purchase, the item is delivered the next time you open the game. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date of the purchase.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
