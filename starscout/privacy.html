Start Scout Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Start Scout (像素星探, "the game") is made by Jie Zhou. The short version: the game does not collect any data about you.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. It does not send your game progress, device information, identifiers, location or any other data to us or to anyone else. Your agency, models and progress are saved only on your device.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (star gem packs, the Scout Monthly Card and Frenzy Ticket packs) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver the item.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

The people in the game

The model portraits in the game are fictional characters created with AI image generation. They do not depict real people.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email jiezhou0731@gmail.com.
