Start Scout Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 (version 1.0.2)

Start Scout (像素星探, "the game") is made by Jie Zhou. The short version: we collect anonymous gameplay statistics, never anything that identifies you. The only third party in the game is Google AdMob, which plays an optional rewarded video when you ask for one.

Information we collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. From version 1.0.2 it sends anonymous gameplay statistics to our own server: a random install ID created by the game (not your device's advertising ID), how your agencies went (weeks, money, rank, levels cleared, scouting and signings), purchases made in the game, rewarded videos watched, the app version, and crash reports. This is used only to balance the game and fix bugs; it is not linked to your identity, not sold and not used for tracking. It contains no name, e-mail, location or contacts. Your save stays on your device. If you are offline, the statistics wait on your device and are sent later; the game works the same either way.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads: double the Starlight an agency earns when it ends, a free scouting trip, or a week's gig income once more, each a few times a day. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one; there are no banners and no ads that play on their own. Google AdMob serves them and may collect your IP address, device type and model, operating system and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud. It would also collect an advertising identifier, but only if you allowed tracking. The game never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so AdMob receives no advertising identifier unless you have granted tracking. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver the item.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

The people in the game

The model portraits in the game are fictional characters created with AI image generation. They do not depict real people.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
