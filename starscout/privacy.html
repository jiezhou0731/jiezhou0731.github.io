Start Scout Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Start Scout (像素星探, "the game") is made by Jie Zhou. In short: we do not collect any data about you. The optional rewarded video ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not send your game progress, device information, identifiers, location or any other data to us. Your agency, models and progress are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads: double the starlight an agency earns when it ends, a free scouting trip, or a week's job income once more, each a few times a day. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one; there are no banners and no ads that play on their own. Google AdMob serves them and may collect your IP address, device type and model, operating system and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud. It would also collect an advertising identifier, but only if you allowed tracking. The game never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so AdMob receives no advertising identifier unless you have granted tracking. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game that a purchase succeeded so it can deliver the item.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

The people in the game

The model portraits in the game are fictional characters created with AI image generation. They do not depict real people.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email jiezhou0731@gmail.com.
