Need help? Email jiezhou0731@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I play?

Tap to throw a knife into the spinning ball. Hit monsters, avoid knives that are already stuck, and don't hit the bunnies. Clear all ten stages of a chapter to beat its boss. In-game help is under Pause → How to Play.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game. Purchases that were interrupted are delivered automatically the next time the game starts.

I reinstalled or got a new device

Open the Shop, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore Purchases. This restores one-time unlocks such as the Starter Pack, Growth Fund and Drake Pack. Gems and other consumable items can't be restored, and game progress is saved only on the device it was played on.
